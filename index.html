Meet Your Coach: Myint

Helping you build a strong, mobile, and resilient body using the ultimate training tool: yourself.

Hi everyone,

My name is Myint. I'm a freelance personal trainer specializing in calisthenics and bodyweight exercise.

For over 10 years, I've practiced calisthenics and longevity protocols. Today, I coach others to build functional strength, improve physical fitness, and cultivate a sustainable, healthy lifestyle.

My Philosophy: Movement for Longevity

My ultimate goal for you is simple: a healthy body and a calm mind for as long as possible.

I believe that any movement is better than no movement. I chose calisthenics because it's efficient, rewarding, and best of all, the world is your gym.

Unlike machine-based gym workouts, calisthenics has a learning curve at first. But with proper guidance, the results are worth it. Soon, moving this way becomes part of your daily life.

The Myth: "You need heavy weights to build muscle"

Many people believe that building a strong, muscular physique requires lifting heavy weights. But that's simply not true.

Your own body is an incredibly powerful training tool. With a structured approach, bodyweight training can effectively build strength, lean muscle, and deep joint mobility all at once.

My Journey: Why I Coach

When I first started, my biggest obstacle was a lack of direction. I wasted a lot of time following random online workouts without any real structure, which slowed my progress and led to unnecessary frustration.

I want to help you skip the frustrating trial-and-error phase.

By giving you a clear plan, we'll reach your fitness goals efficiently and safely. My focus is long-term consistency: building habits that last, avoiding injuries, and actually enjoying the process.

Who This Is For

This coaching is designed specifically for you if:

  • Freedom in training: Build a strong body anywhere without expensive gym memberships.
  • Functional strength: Protect your body and focus on longevity, not just lifting heavy.
  • Clear structure: No more guessing. Get a personalized, step-by-step program.
  • All fitness levels: Whether you cannot do a push-up yet or want to master advanced movements, we start where you are.

About

Hi everyone

My name is Myint. I'm a freelance personal trainer specializing in calisthenics—the beautiful art of bodyweight exercise.

For over 10 years, I've practiced calisthenics and longevity protocols. Today, I coach others to build functional strength, improve physical fitness, and cultivate a sustainable, healthy lifestyle.

Philosophy

Movement for longevity

My ultimate goal for you is simple: a healthy body and a calm mind for as long as possible.

I believe that any movement is better than no movement. I chose calisthenics because it is efficient, deeply rewarding, and best of all—the world is your gym.

Unlike machine-based gym workouts, calisthenics can have a slight learning curve at the beginning. But with the right guidance at the start, the results are worth ten times the effort. Before you know it, moving this way will become an effortless, joyful part of your daily life.

The myth

"You need heavy weights to build muscle"

Many people believe that building a strong, muscular physique requires lifting heavy weights. But that's simply not true.

Your own body is an incredibly powerful training tool. With a structured approach, bodyweight training can effectively build strength, lean muscle, and deep joint mobility all at once.

My journey

Why I coach

When I first started, my biggest obstacle was a lack of direction. I wasted a lot of time following random online workouts without any real structure, which slowed my progress and led to unnecessary frustration.

I want to help you skip that frustrating trial-and-error phase.

By giving you a clear, personalized plan, we will work together to reach your fitness goals efficiently, safely, and with confidence. My focus isn't just on quick, short-term fixes. It is about long-term consistency—helping you build habits that last, avoid injuries, and actually enjoy the process of becoming a healthier individual.

Who this is for

Built for you if…

  • Freedom in your training You want to build a strong, capable body anywhere—without being locked into an expensive gym membership.
  • Tired of joint pain You want functional strength that protects and heals your body, focusing on longevity rather than just lifting heavy.
  • A clear roadmap You are done wasting time guessing what to do and want a step-by-step, personalized structure.
  • Any fitness level Whether you cannot do a single push-up yet, or you are looking to master advanced bodyweight movements, we start exactly where you are.
